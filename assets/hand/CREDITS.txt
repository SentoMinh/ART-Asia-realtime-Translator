Hand model
-----------
Source file: hand2_1.obj
Credit: omoideshabou

Bundled here as a reduced preview mesh (omoideshabou.npz) for the VR wrist
settings dialog. Not redistributed as the original full OBJ.

Prepared from the user-supplied OBJ with tools/prepare_hand.py. The preview
straightens and scales the mesh, clusters nearby vertices, and mirrors it
for the other wrist. The bundled mesh contains 4,531 triangles.
